## Description (max 4000 characters)
```
Sleep Suivour turns your health habits into an adventure. Track fasting, sleep, and meals — then watch your progress deal damage to monsters, earn loot, and climb the leaderboard.

YOUR HEALTH, GAMIFIED
Start a fasting timer and choose your goal: 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, or custom. Log powerups like water, coffee, exercise, and walks as you go. When you sleep, track your hours and quality. Every healthy choice builds your Heart Points — a real-time score split into Brawn, Brain, and Bloat.

SLAY MONSTERS WITH YOUR HABITS
Your fasting and sleep hours deal real damage to bosses like the Visceral Fat Beast and Insulin Dragon. Earn streak bonuses, skill multipliers, and powerup damage. The longer you fast and the better you sleep, the harder you hit.

Unlock collectible items as you progress. Level up skills like hydration, exercise, and nutrition. Every walk, every glass of water, every good meal earns XP.

SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
Sign in with Google or Apple to sync your data across all your devices in real time. Your progress is always safe in the cloud.

APPLE HEALTH INTEGRATION
Sleep Suivour can write your sleep sessions to Apple Health, keeping all your health data in one place.

Sleep Suivour is built by someone who believes health should be fun, not punishing. Every feature is designed to encourage — never shame. Whether you fast for 12 hours or 24, sleep 6 hours or 8, every effort counts.

Your companion Sui (the Sleep God) is cheering you on. Let's slay some monsters.
```

## App Review Notes (for Apple's review team)

```
TESTING THE APP:

1. SIGN IN: Tap the Settings tab, then sign in with Google or Apple. A username prompt will appear — enter any 3-20 character alphanumeric name.

2. FASTING: Go to the Play tab. Tap "Start Fast" and select a goal (e.g., 16:8). The timer starts counting. You can log powerups (water, coffee, exercise, walk) during the fast. Tap "Stop Fast" to end the session.

3. SLEEP: On the Play tab, switch to the Sleep sub-tab. Tap "Start Sleep" and select a goal. Tap "Stop Sleep" to log the session.

4. EATING: On the Play tab, switch to the Eating sub-tab. Log a meal and select quality indicators (protein, fiber, home-cooked, etc.).

5. MONSTER BATTLES: Go to the Battles tab. Your fasting and sleep hours deal damage to monsters (Visceral Fat Beast, Insulin Dragon). Damage is calculated from your session history.

6. STATS: The Stats tab shows trends, history, and leaderboards.

7. HEALTHKIT: The app requests HealthKit permission to write sleep data. This can be tested by completing a sleep session.

8. CROSS-DEVICE SYNC: Sign in on two devices with the same account. Changes sync in real time via Firebase.

No demo account is needed — the app works immediately with any Google or Apple account. All features are available without a subscription in this version.

The app does not use any background location, microphone, camera, or contacts. HealthKit is used only to write sleep session data (start time, end time, duration).
```
