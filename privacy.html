1. Who We Are

Sleep Suivour ("the App") is developed and operated by Lurio Neli ("we", "us", "our"). The App is available as a web application and as a native iOS application.

For privacy questions, contact us at: sleepsuivourprivacy@gmail.com

2. Age Requirement

Sleep Suivour is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at sleepsuivourprivacy@gmail.com and we will promptly delete that information.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive:

  • Email address (from your sign-in provider; Apple users may choose to use a private relay email)
  • Display name (from your sign-in provider)
  • Profile photo URL (from your sign-in provider)
  • Unique user identifier (UID) — a unique ID assigned by Firebase Authentication, used to associate your data with your account

You also create a username (3-20 alphanumeric characters) which is used for leaderboards and the community forum. Usernames cannot currently be changed after creation.

3.2 Gamification Data

The App includes gamification features. We store:

  • Heart Points scores (Brawn, Brain, Bloat metrics)
  • Collectible items unlocked and equipped
  • Monster battle progress
  • Living Life mode usage history

3.3 Public Content

Certain data you create is visible to other authenticated users:

  • Leaderboard entries — your username, Heart Points, total XP, skill scores, and equipped item
  • Forum posts — your username, post content (max 280 characters), and equipped item. Your user identifier is also stored with each post for ownership purposes.
  • Forum likes — which posts you have liked

You can delete your own forum posts at any time. When you delete your account or all data, your leaderboard entries and forum posts are also removed.

3.4 Push Notifications (iOS App Only)

The iOS app may request permission to send push notifications. If you grant permission, a device token is generated by Apple. This token is currently stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

3.5 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Browsing history outside the App
  • Contacts, calendar, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Precise calorie counts, weight, or body composition
  • Heart rate, blood pressure, or medical diagnostic data

4. Health and Wellness Data

Important: Your health and wellness data is used exclusively to provide the App's core tracking features. It is never used for advertising, marketing, data mining, or sale to third parties.

The core purpose of the App is to track your health habits. We store the following data that you voluntarily provide:

  • Fasting sessions — start time, end time, goal duration, and powerups logged (water, coffee, tea, exercise, walks, etc.)
  • Sleep sessions — start time, end time, goal duration, and sleep quality
  • Meal logging — meal timestamps and quality indicators (protein, fiber, home-cooked, etc.)
  • Skills and experience points — progression data tied to your health activities
  • Biological sex (optional) — used for personalized guidance
  • Menstrual cycle data (optional, opt-in) — last period start date and cycle length

All health data listed above is classified as sensitive personal information. It is collected only with your active participation (you must manually start sessions and log activities). We use this data solely to provide the features you interact with. It is never shared with third parties for their own purposes, and never used for advertising or profiling.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide the App's features — tracking fasting, sleep, and meals; calculating scores; unlocking collectibles
  • Sync your data across devices — so you can access your progress on any browser or device
  • Display leaderboards and forum content — using only your chosen username and scores
  • Prevent abuse — rate-limiting forum posts and validating data integrity

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, automated decision-making, data mining, or sale to third parties.

6. How We Store Your Information

6.1 Local Storage

The App stores your data locally in your browser's localStorage. This data remains on your device and persists until you clear your browser data, delete your data through the App, or uninstall the App.

6.2 Cloud Storage

When you sign in, your data is synced to Google Firebase Realtime Database. Firebase servers are located in the United States. Your data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/WSS connections.

Firebase Security Rules ensure that each user can only read and write their own data (with the exception of public leaderboard entries and forum posts).

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data Shared
Google Firebase Authentication User sign-in (Google and Apple providers) Email, display name, user identifier, authentication tokens
Google Firebase Realtime Database Cloud data storage and real-time sync All App data (health sessions, scores, forum posts)
Google Fonts (web version only) Typography (Inter font family) Standard HTTP headers only; no personal data. The iOS app bundles fonts locally and does not contact Google Fonts.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting tools, or any other third-party trackers.

Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Google provides equivalent protection of user data under the Firebase Data Processing Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

8. Apple HealthKit (iOS App Only)

With your explicit permission, the iOS app writes sleep session data (start time, end time, duration) to Apple HealthKit.

  • HealthKit data is stored locally on your device by Apple and is not sent to our servers or any third party.
  • We do not read data from HealthKit.
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.
  • HealthKit data is not stored in iCloud by the App.
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Health.

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Public features — Your username, scores, and forum posts are visible to other authenticated users as described in Section 3.3.
  • Service providers — Google Firebase processes your data as described in Section 7. No other service providers have access to your data.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

In the preceding 12 months, we have not sold or shared personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

10.1 Retention

  • Cloud data is retained indefinitely in Firebase until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Local data is retained until you clear your browser storage, uninstall the App, or delete your data through the App.
  • Forum posts are retained until you delete them individually or delete your account.
  • Leaderboard entries are retained until you delete your data or your account.

10.2 Deletion Options

You have the following deletion options available in the App's Account tab:

  • Delete individual forum posts — Remove specific posts you have created.
  • Delete All Data — Permanently removes all your data (fasting history, sleep history, scores, forum posts, leaderboard entries) from both your device and our servers. Your account remains active but empty.
  • Delete Account — Permanently deletes your account and all associated data from both your device and our servers. This action is irreversible.

When you delete your account or data, all associated public content (leaderboard entries and forum posts) is also removed.

Note: Selective deletion of individual fasting sessions, sleep sessions, or other non-forum data is not currently available. The "Delete All Data" function removes all data at once.

12. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on Firebase servers in the United States. If you are located outside the United States (including Canada, the EEA, and the UK), your data is transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We rely on Google's data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses for lawful cross-border data transfers.

13. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data transmitted between your device and Firebase uses HTTPS/WSS encryption
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict data access to authenticated users and their own records
  • Input sanitization prevents cross-site scripting (XSS) and injection attacks
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers restrict resource loading
  • Forum posts are rate-limited and URL-filtered to prevent spam and abuse
  • Username validation prevents impersonation
  • Destructive actions (data deletion, account deletion) require multiple confirmations

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Firebase Authentication uses browser storage (localStorage and session tokens) solely for maintaining your sign-in session. No third-party cookies are set. We do not use any analytics or tracking technologies.
