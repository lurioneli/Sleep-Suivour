METABOLIC MARKERS

Insulin Level HIGH

Estimated from hours fasted. Insulin drops steadily after your last meal and reaches baseline around 12-16h. Lower = more fat burning.

Glycogen Stores FULL

Liver sugar reserves. Your body burns through these in the first ~12h of fasting. Once depleted, you switch to burning fat.

Ketone Production MINIMAL

Your liver makes ketones from fat when glycogen runs out (~12h+). Ketones fuel your brain and signal cellular cleanup. Higher = deeper fasting.

AMPK (Repair) vs mTOR (Growth) mTOR ▲

Two competing cellular signals. mTOR (red) drives growth when fed. AMPK (green) activates repair & autophagy during fasting. The balance shifts as you fast longer.

Growth Hormone 1x baseline

GH protects muscle and boosts fat burning. Rises 2-3x after 24h fasting, up to 5x after 48h. This is why fasting preserves muscle!

WARNING: 24+ Hour Fast

Your stomach has begun to shrink. Proceed with caution, adventurer.

  • 1. Sip broth slowly - 30 minute minimum cast time
  • 2. Wait 3-4 hours - Cooldown before solid food
  • 3. Protein & fiber - Drink your food, do not rush
  • 4. Post-meal walk - Activates digestion buff
  • 5. Eat plenty of fiber - Vegetables, fruits & whole grains to prevent constipation

Constipation is common after 24+ hour fasts. Load up on fiber-rich whole foods in your refeeding meals.

DANGER ZONE: 36+ Hour Fast

Your stomach has shrunk significantly. This is a high-level challenge!

  • 1. Sip broth every few hours - Do not rush this phase!
  • 2. Wait 8+ HOURS - Extended cooldown before solids
  • 3. Small portions only - Drink your food completely
  • 4. Gentle walk after - Soft activation of systems
  • 5. Fiber is critical - Vegetables, fruits & whole grains to restore gut motility

Constipation is very common after 36+ hour fasts. Your gut has slowed down — prioritise fiber-rich whole foods to get things moving again.

Fasting & Your Heart — The Reset Button

Insulin resistance is the root cause of heart disease. Fasting is the cure.

  • > Reverses insulin resistance — The #1 driver of cardiovascular disease. Fasting drops insulin levels, letting your body heal
  • > Reduces triglycerides — High triglycerides damage arteries. Fasting burns them as fuel, improving your lipid profile
  • > Raises HDL cholesterol — The protective cholesterol that cleans your arteries increases with fasting
  • > Activates autophagy — Your body recycles damaged cells and arterial plaque. A deep clean for your cardiovascular system
  • > Targets visceral fat — The dangerous fat around your organs that directly increases heart attack risk
  • > Reduces chronic inflammation — Inflammation damages blood vessels and causes plaque buildup. Fasting calms it

Eating for Your Heart — Fuel, Not Poison

What you eat between fasts determines whether you heal or undo your progress.

  • > Fatty fish — Salmon, sardines, mackerel. Omega-3s reduce inflammation and protect arteries
  • > Extra virgin olive oil — Anti-inflammatory monounsaturated fat. Use it generously
  • > Leafy greens — Spinach, kale, arugula. Rich in nitrates that lower blood pressure naturally
  • > Nuts & seeds — Walnuts, almonds, flaxseed. Lower LDL cholesterol and reduce inflammation
  • > Turmeric & ginger — Powerful anti-inflammatory spices that protect blood vessels
  • > Berries — Blueberries, strawberries. Packed with antioxidants that prevent arterial damage

Avoid: Seed oils (canola, soybean, sunflower), refined carbs, processed foods, excess sugar. These drive insulin resistance and inflammation — the true causes of heart disease.

Sleep & Your Heart — The Night Shift Repair Crew

Your heart heals while you sleep. Poor sleep is a direct risk factor for heart disease.

  • > Blood pressure drops — During deep sleep, your blood pressure falls 10-20%. This nightly dip is essential for arterial health
  • > Heart rate recovery — Your resting heart rate drops during sleep, giving your cardiovascular system time to rest and repair
  • > Cortisol regulation — Sleep resets cortisol levels. Chronic high cortisol from poor sleep raises blood pressure and damages arteries
  • > Inflammation reduction — Sleep deprivation increases C-reactive protein and inflammatory markers linked to heart attacks
  • > Insulin sensitivity — Even one night of poor sleep increases insulin resistance. Chronic sleep loss multiplies cardiovascular risk
  • > 7-9 hours minimum — Less than 6 hours consistently raises heart disease risk significantly. Quality matters as much as quantity
Fasting, eating right, and sleeping well — the three pillars of a strong heart.

Bone Broth — The Ultimate Fast-Breaker

Rich in collagen, amino acids & minerals. The ideal first food after an extended fast.

  1. 1. Get bones — Beef marrow, chicken carcass, or fish bones
  2. 2. Add acid — 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar (draws minerals from bones)
  3. 3. Cover with water — Fill pot, bring to boil, then reduce to low
  4. 4. Simmer 12-24 hours — Low heat, patience is the secret ingredient
  5. 5. Last hour: add veg — Onion, garlic, celery, salt & pepper
  6. 6. Strain & store — Fridge for 5 days or freeze in portions

High Fiber Foods — Prevent Post-Fast Constipation

After 24+ hour fasts, your gut slows down. Fiber gets things moving again.

  • > Vegetables — Broccoli, Brussels sprouts, artichoke, sweet potato
  • > Legumes — Lentils, chickpeas, black beans
  • > Fruits — Avocado, berries, pears, apples (with skin)
  • > Whole grains — Oats, quinoa
  • > Seeds — Chia seeds, flaxseed

Gut Health Foods — Rebuild Your Microbiome

Fasting resets your gut. Feed the good bacteria when you refeed.

  • > Fermented foods — Sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, natural yogurt
  • > Prebiotics — Garlic, onion, asparagus, bananas
  • > Bone broth — Collagen repairs and strengthens gut lining
  • > Apple cider vinegar — Supports stomach acid production

Anti-Inflammatory Foods — Fight the Root Cause

Chronic inflammation is the root of heart disease, diabetes & more. Eat to fight it.

  • > Fatty fish — Salmon, sardines, mackerel (omega-3 rich)
  • > Leafy greens — Spinach, kale, Swiss chard
  • > Berries — Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries
  • > Spices — Turmeric, ginger (powerful anti-inflammatories)
  • > Healthy fats — Extra virgin olive oil, walnuts, almonds

Avoid: Seed oils, processed sugar, refined carbs — these CAUSE inflammation.

What is Visceral Fat?

Visceral fat is the hidden fat that wraps around your internal organs. Unlike subcutaneous fat (under the skin), visceral fat is metabolically active and releases harmful substances.

Male Profile

Female Profile

Why It's Dangerous:

  • • Releases inflammatory chemicals
  • • Increases risk of heart disease
  • • Linked to type 2 diabetes
  • • Associated with certain cancers
  • • Causes fatty liver disease

How Fasting Helps:

  • • After 12+ hours: Body starts burning stored fat
  • • After 16+ hours: Autophagy begins cleaning cells
  • • After 18+ hours: Deep fat burning mode activated
  • • Visceral fat is often burned FIRST during fasting!

What is Insulin Resistance?

Insulin resistance is when your cells stop responding properly to insulin, the hormone that helps glucose enter cells for energy. Think of it like a lock and key - the key (insulin) no longer fits the lock (cell receptors) properly.

The Insulin Resistance Dragon is weakened by fasting, good sleep, and clean eating!

Why It's Dangerous:

  • • Leads to high blood sugar levels
  • • Precursor to type 2 diabetes
  • • Causes weight gain (especially belly fat)
  • • Increases heart disease risk
  • • Causes fatigue and brain fog

How Fasting Fights the Dragon:

  • • Fasting directly lowers insulin levels — the dragon's fuel source
  • • After 12+ hours, insulin drops enough for fat burning to begin
  • • Extended fasts reverse insulin resistance at the cellular level
  • • Each hour fasted deals 5 damage to the Dragon

How Sleep Fights the Dragon:

  • • Just ONE night of poor sleep can reduce insulin sensitivity by 25%!
  • • 7-8 hours of sleep helps maintain healthy insulin levels
  • • Deep sleep is when your body repairs insulin receptors
  • • Good sleep reduces cortisol, which improves insulin sensitivity
  • • Each hour of sleep deals 15 damage to the Dragon

How Eating Well Fights the Dragon:

  • • Good eating powerups boost Dragon damage up to 1.5x
  • • Bad eating (junk food, eating too fast) weakens your attacks
  • • Protein, fiber, broth, and slow eating are your best weapons
